Using OrderStash

  1. Open Amazon’s Your Orders page. Available years and the order count come from that page.
  2. Choose the current page or a year. Save invoice PDFs is the main action; CSV is available alongside it.
  3. Keep Chrome open. You can close the controls while exporting; reopen them to see progress, pause or resume.

Open Orders to search saved summaries and check each order’s PDF and CSV export status. These formats are tracked separately. Exported badges show completed downloads, even if you later move or delete the files.

Summary collection reads history pages only. Details, product pages and tracking pages are visited only if you select those options. Invoice pages are visited only for PDF export.

OrderStash takes a five-minute break at its visit budget, then continues automatically. Pause cancels that continuation. Amazon blocks and sign-in challenges stop for your review. Resume finds an open Orders tab without a separate refresh step.

OrderStash saves your own order records locally. Amazon's terms distinguish third-party use of account information and separately restrict automated extraction tools. Personal recordkeeping does not establish permission for every automated method. Sign-in, challenges and rate limits stop automated visits.

Amazon’s Conditions of Use

Orders and export history stay in this Chrome profile. No account, subscription, telemetry or OrderStash server. Downloads are saved under Downloads/OrderStash.

Why does Chrome say “debugging this browser”?

Native PDF printing uses Chrome’s debugger API. OrderStash attaches it to its dedicated invoice tab, and detaches when the export finishes or pauses. Opening a different tab does not move that attachment.

The debugger permission itself is powerful and is not restricted to Amazon by Chrome. OrderStash’s code limits its use to invoice printing; its normal page scripts run only on the supported Amazon sites.

Amazon layouts vary. Check important records against Amazon. Some seller PDF invoices or multiple invoices for one order may need manual export.
